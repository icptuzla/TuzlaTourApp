## 🇬🇧 ENGLISH VERSION

### 🌟 Overview & Objective
Welcome to **Tuzla Tour Quest**, an interactive, gamified Augmented Reality (AR) and 3D map exploration game designed to guide tourists and local visitors through the rich history, cultural monuments, and top attractions of Tuzla, Bosnia and Herzegovina.

Your objective is to complete all **4 Quest Phases** by visiting landmarks across the city, scanning official QR codes or approaching within 5 meters, unlocking exclusive blockchain rewards, trophies, and merchant discount vouchers along the way.

---

### 🗺️ Screen Layout: 60 / 40 Dual Interactive View
When opening the **AR Guide & Navigation** mode in the app, your screen is divided into two optimized zones:
1. **Top 40% (Interactive 3D Map):** Displays the live 3D map of Tuzla (`MapQuestView`), showing your current GPS position, route path polyline, and POI markers.
   - **3D Gesture Controls:** Use two fingers to slide up/down to adjust pitch (map angle), rotate with two fingers, or hold right-click on desktop to adjust viewing angles.
2. **Bottom 60% (AR Viewport & Guide Dashboard):** Contains the live camera pass-through feed, dynamic 3D AR markers, target locking reticle, side direction chevrons, turn-by-turn GPS instructions, and the interactive landmark dashboard.

### 🎨 Visual Marker Legend & POI States
To keep your map clean and focused on your active goals:
- **Active Phase Targets:** Rendered as bright, pulsing 3D color pins matching their unique landmark color palette.
- **Completed Targets:** Landmarks finished in previous phases automatically transform into **grayscale, 40% transparent, blurred markers** (`opacity-40 grayscale blur-[1px]`), so you always know where you've been while focusing on remaining goals.

---

### 🔓 How to Unlock Landmarks
You can unlock any landmark using two methods:
1. **Method A (QR Code Scanning):** Tap the **QR Code** button in the app HUD or AR panel and point your camera at the official Tuzla Tour QR plaque at the site.
2. **Method B (5-Meter GPS Geofence):** Walk within 5 meters of the landmark's coordinates with active GPS location services. The app will automatically prompt you with an automated unlock notification.
